Pick Your Fish

Dalmatia, Croatia

Ordering along this coast follows a form, and it is worth knowing before you sit down. The fish is not on the menu — it is in a case by the door, whole, on ice, priced by the kilo. You go and look, you point, it is weighed in front of you, and the kitchen’s entire job from that moment is to do as little to it as possible.

From the same trip — Dubrovnik Before the Ships and Hvar Runs on Boats and Out to the Elaphiti.
