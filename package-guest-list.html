Package Guest List

Every guest booked on a package, across all groups — with a repeat-guest badge (R4 = four stays; click it for the years) and a pre-trip readiness bar showing how far each guest has got with their profile, airfare, fishing licence and the rest. Click any bar for the breakdown; click a chip to chase just that group.

Maps to Andy's schema → guests are persons linked to this trip via group_persons + trip_profiles. The repeat count & years come from the person's stay history (all trips that resolved to the same person via matching) — R<n> = distinct stay years. Editing here writes the trip profile; identity stays on the person.
Trip readiness is derived, never stored — each step reads the guest's own trip_profile fields (contact, address, sizing, emergency contact, health answers), their flight legs, and whether a fishing-license asset exists. Same record the Guest Portal writes, so the bar moves the moment a guest saves.
