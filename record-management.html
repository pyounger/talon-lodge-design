Talon Lodge Platform · Master-Record Management

One person. One record. Every visit, forever.

People arrive through three different doors, but the system keeps exactly one master record per human being. Every arrival is matched against who's already known; matches fold in, new people get a fresh record, and uncertain cases go to a human. From then on, a person's whole history — every group, every trip, every role — lives on that single record. This is the spine of the entire platform.

How a record enters and resolves

Three intake channels → one matching engine → one master Person record.

The matching decision

What the engine does with each incoming candidate.

TierSignalActionWhy
1Exact email match (normalized)Auto-linkEmail is the strongest identity signal — same address, same person.
2Exact phone + nickname-equivalent name (Bob = Robert)Auto-linkTwo corroborating signals; nickname table resolves “Phil / Philip”.
3Nickname-equiv name + same last name, no email/phoneReview queuePlausible but unproven — a human confirms before merging.
—No matching signalNew recordA genuinely new person; a fresh master record is created.
Safety: every merge — automatic or human-confirmed — records the losing and surviving states and the exact records it re-pointed, so it can be undone precisely. Merges are the only action that rewrites data across records at once, so they are the only action with full reversibility.

One person, many roles over time

Role belongs to a person’s membership in a group — not to the person. So a guest can become a lead, and the record shows both.

How it stays one record: in 2025 Phil’s booking runs through the same matching engine, hits his 2019 email (Tier 1), and auto-links to the existing person instead of creating a duplicate. A new group membership is added with role = Lead; the 2019 Guest membership is untouched. Nothing is ever overwritten — history only accumulates.
