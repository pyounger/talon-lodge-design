Daily boat & activity report

The dock-and-spa run sheet for a single day — every assignment by individual guest. Boats list their captain, deckhand and seated guests; massage lists each appointment by time with therapist & treatment; adventures list each guide’s guests. A guest booked on an adventure is pulled off the boat for that day (the activity takes priority). Pulls live from Boat Assignments & Approvals.

Reads talon_boat_seating (boat assignments), talon_bookings_v1 (approved activities & massage), talon_team_v1 (crew) and talon_packages_v1. Assign boats in Boat Assignments and approve activities in Approvals, then a date fills in here. Beverage orders will appear once captured on the guest profile.
