Availability & Scheduling

Pick a package — its real arrival→departure dates become the calendar — choose a group, then tick the individual guests on each booking (every activity attaches to the person, not just the group). Bookings save against the actual date, so groups the same week compete for the same guides; the engine assigns the lead guide first. Boats seated in Boat Assignments show on the master calendar, and a booked activity pulls that guest off the boat for the day.
