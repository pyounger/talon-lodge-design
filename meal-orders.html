Meal & Beverage Orders

Each night, take the next morning's breakfast and the day's beverage order — per guest. Night 1 → Day 1 (first activity day). Breakfast is free choice from that day's menu; beverages roll onto the boat‑stocking list by whichever boat the guest is on that day.

Maps to Andy's schema → per‑person meal_order (trip_id, group, person, day, breakfast_item_ids[], notes) + beverage_order (person, day, beverage_id, qty). Boat stocking aggregates beverage_order by the person's boat assignment for that day.
