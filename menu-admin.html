Menu Items

The breakfast & lunch menu by package day. Regular items are the mains a guest chooses from; Custom items are the mods, subs & sides (e.g. Sub Sausage, Wheat, No Lox). The per-guest order (taken the night before) picks a main plus any custom mods for that morning.
