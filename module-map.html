Talon Lodge Platform · Capability Map

Every module, arranged by the guest's journey

The system reads left to right as one guest lifecycle — from setting up inventory to the post-stay survey. Every phase writes to a single People & Trip record, and every phase stands on a shared layer of platform services. Nothing here is a silo.
