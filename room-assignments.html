Room Assignments

The reservation assigns the room(s) to the group. Here you place each individual guest into one of the group's rooms. Over‑capacity is accepted and flagged (never blocked). Every assignment attaches to the guest, so it flows to the daily report and rooming list.

Maps to Andy's schema → group room_allocation (trip_id, group, room_id) from the reservation + per‑person room_assignment (person_id, room_id). Rooms lookup: rooms (id, name, building, capacity).
