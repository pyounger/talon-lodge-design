Check-in, payment & fish — who decides, where, and with what tool

Two decisions belong to the group lead — who pays for add-ons and how each guest's fish is handled. Each is made in the portal, or completed at check-in if it wasn't done before arrival. When the lead makes a guest responsible for a payment or for part of the fish, that guest's own portal gets the tasks to confirm — including a required FedEx risk disclaimer. This maps every step to its tool and status.

What to connect next

  1. Embed the three tools in the Guest Portal, and expose the same two lead tools at the front desk — so “in the portal” and “at check-in if not decided” are literally the same tool in two places.
  2. One shared record. The lead's choices must drive the guest's confirmation tasks and the billing folios from the same data — so a guest only sees confirmations for what they were actually assigned. (Standalone prototypes can't share across origins; the production DB / one-origin test hub closes this.)
  3. Extend Fish Processing to (a) let the lead assign cost responsibility per share, and (b) consume each guest's take / FedEx choice for the processor and shipping list.
  4. Check-in “not done yet?” gate. On arrival, if the portal is incomplete, the desk runs the very same Payment Responsibility, Fish, and Confirmation tools to finish it with the guest.
  5. Log the FedEx disclaimer acceptance (guest, timestamp, wording version) on the guest record for liability, and surface it on the processing sheet.
  6. Finalize rates. Real spa treatment prices, activity rates, alcohol line items, and the fish-processing fee so folio totals are live, not indicative.
