What to look at

  • Does it read as part of the page? The form takes the site's ground, accent and type, so the seam should be the booking bar, not a colour change.
  • Phone width. Switch to Phone — the date pair, the four date-flexibility buttons and the interest chips all reflow to one column. This is where embedded forms usually break.
  • The Bluff House shows the fixed party of six instead of a stepper.
  • Magnus is deliberately unstyled, pending brand assets.

Known gaps in this reproduction

  • Talon's hero is photography; here it is a tone band. The gold wordmark, rule and booking bar are placed as measured.
  • The real sites' typefaces could not be identified from screenshots — Cormorant Garamond and Lora stand in.
