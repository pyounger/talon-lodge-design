How it works · 1

The system reads left-to-right as one guest's journey

Six phases — set up the inventory, sell it, prepare the guest, run the stay, follow up, and merge the history. Each phase writes back to one People & Trip record; nothing is a silo.

How it works · 2

Everything hangs off one person

A guest is matched to a single person master record, then accumulates groups, trips, per-trip profiles, and day-by-day asset assignments over years of visits. History only accumulates — it's never overwritten.

Check-in, payment & fish

The lead decides; each guest confirms

At check-in — in the portal, or at the desk if it wasn't done before arrival — the group lead sets who pays for each guest's activities, spa & massage, alcohol and fish processing, and how the fish is handled. When a guest is made responsible, their own portal gets the tasks to confirm.

⚠ FedEx fish — required risk disclaimer

If a guest chooses to ship their catch by FedEx, they must read and accept a disclaimer before confirming: shipments can be delayed and fish can thaw, spoil or be lost — Talon Lodge and the fish-processing company do not absorb this risk. Acceptance (who & when) is recorded on the guest's record.

For the team to decide

Open decisions & next steps

  1. Finalize rates. Real spa-treatment prices (Denali Deep, Aurora Refresher, Sitka Swedish + base 50-min), activity rates, alcohol, and the fish-processing fee — so folio totals are live, not indicative.
  2. Billing granularity. Does checkout bill per guest or per group (lead settles)? And do we add a tax/fee line (Sitka) to the total?
  3. Embed the tools in the portal and expose the lead tools at the front desk — so "in the portal" and "at check-in" are the same tool in two places.
  4. One shared record. Wire the lead's choices to drive each guest's confirmations and the billing folios from one dataset (proven in the shared demo; the production DB closes it).
  5. Extend Fish Processing to assign per-share cost responsibility and to consume each guest's take / FedEx choice for the processor and shipping list.
  6. Begin the Angular / Laravel port — stand up the schema, the assignment scheduler, and the guest-portal magic-link; the prototypes are the behavioral spec.

Where to review the live versions

Every report and tool in this document is also a live, interactive page (private links), and each carries a print PDF in the talon-lodge-design repo. The shared demo shows the payment → confirmation → billing flow updating live from one dataset.
