Boat assignments

Assign guests to boats for their sportfishing — pick the package date, then a group, then a boat. Each boat seats up to its set capacity (from Team — a boat may be 6, 4 or 2); groups can share a boat, and if a group doesn’t fully fit you choose which guests go on it and place the rest elsewhere. Any day a group has a scheduled activity, that activity takes priority and those guests drop off the boat for the day.
